Shadows show in the dark theme. A black shadow vanished on the dark page, so the dark theme now draws the elevation levels with a light shadow, a soft glow that grows with the level. The levels are built from new theme tokens `--s-shadow-color`, `--s-shadow-key-opacity` and `--s-shadow-ambient-opacity` (also accepted by `createTheme`); the light theme looks as before.
