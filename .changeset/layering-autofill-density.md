Layout fixes. `--s-z-dropdown` moves from 1000 to 1500, the popover level: lists of `SSelect`, `SAutocomplete`, `SDropdownMenu`, `SContextMenu` and `SMenubar` no longer open under an `SDialog` or `SDrawer` and its overlay. Floating labels rise over a value filled by browser autofill. `STabs` scrolls a list of tabs that does not fit its container and scrolls a tab activated from outside into view. `SNumberField` and `SPinInput` get a `size` prop, so the density presets reach them.
